Read this before trusting the tab

  • Rhythm is not transcribed. Notes are placed by when they start, in proportion to time; there are no bars or note values.
  • Confidences are uncalibrated. The decoder ranks its notes, and the ones it is least sure of are marked, but its numbers are not probabilities: end to end on GuitarSet's test players its calibration error is 0.1841 (ADR 0057). Use the marks as a ranking, a list of places to check by ear.
  • Isolated guitar only. One guitar, no band, no vocals. Full songs are a later phase.
  • What to expect. On EGDB, recordings neither the transcriber nor the decoder was tuned on, the tab scores an F1 of 0.4547 when a note counts only if its start (within 50 ms), its pitch and its string all match the player's (end-to-end E2, ADR 0057). Given the true notes instead of transcribed ones, the decoder alone scores 0.6762 (oracle E2, ADR 0054): transcription errors cost 0.22 of the score, and strings chosen differently from the player's cost 0.32.
