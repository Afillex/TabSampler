## Guitar-TECHS's labels, checked against its audio (Task 2)

`data/guitartechs.py` reads a take's MIDI, one track per string (`e B G D A E`); fret = pitch −
the string's open pitch. `scripts/check_guitartechs.py` checks the labels against the
direct-input audio, with GuitarSet's player 00 as the control (onset lag +11.6 ms — the
measure reads a frame late — and **0.949** of its pitches confirmed by the audio: the constant-Q
energy over 30–150 ms after the onset beats a semitone either side).

- **Single notes (players 1 and 2):** 279 notes, all confirmed, none overlapping, none dropped.
- **Player 3's musical excerpts:** 12 takes, 8.5 minutes, 1,940 notes. Per take, 0.54–0.92
  confirmed; 484 notes start while the string's previous note still sounds.
- **What those are:** every overlap is 3–4 ms and changes pitch — the pickup's note-off arriving
  just after the next note-on, harmless once each note's end is cut at the next onset. The
  unconfirmed notes are mostly very short: median 53 ms, against 402 ms for confirmed ones; 247
  of the 478 last under 60 ms, against 65 of the 1,462 confirmed. That is the pickup's pitch
  tracking emitting glitch notes. A duration floor would remove about half the bad labels and
  4% of the good ones; the rule waits for the scales, chords and techniques, still downloading.

## Player 2's chords, and the label delay per take (ADR 0052)

Player 2's chord labels are clean (93–100% confirmed) but split in timing: 15 of 28 takes lag
like the rest of player 2 (~12 ms late), 13 read 46–67 ms **early**. A delay per player (ADR
0051, written before those chords arrived) would have moved half of player 2's chord windows off
their attacks, so the delay is now measured per take from its own audio (`onset_lag`, ADR 0052);
training prints every take's. ADR 0052 was committed with miscounted takes (14/14) and corrected
in the next commit to 15/13 — a factual fix, not a change of decision.

## Task 5, run 1 — the classifier on real electric audio

98 usable takes; 1,536 pickup glitches dropped; 14,788 training notes, 1,527 on player 3. Best
epoch 1 of 4 (held-out NLL 1.2187); it overfits at once.

| per-note string accuracy | Guitar-TECHS classifier | SynthTab's | chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guitar-TECHS player 3 (DI) | 0.3739 | 0.3628 | 0.2657 |
| GuitarSet player 00 (mic) | 0.4365 | 0.4736 | 0.2758 |

**H1 not supported** (0.011 ahead, no interval, prediction 0.55–0.75 failed); **H2 held**.
What it suggests: SynthTab's classifier is *worse* on real electric guitar (0.36) than on
GuitarSet's acoustic one (0.47), which points at rendered-against-real rather than
acoustic-against-electric as what costs it — though player 3 is also another player, another
guitar and harder music than GuitarSet's, so this is a hint, not a measurement of the confound. And 15k notes of drills from two players do not teach a classifier
more than 100k rendered ones. Player 3's labels cap both figures: 14% of its pitches are not
confirmed by the audio after cleaning (ADR 0051).

Ege downloaded EGDB in a browser. Two things went wrong before the figure, both stated:

EGDB, 240 clips, direct input: the default decoder oracle **0.6762**; with the electric evidence
**0.7200 (+0.0438)** — H5 held, the prediction (−0.02 to +0.04) failed high by 0.004; end to end

electric-to-acoustic gap: 0.6762 against 0.6819. ADR 0054 writes Phase 4 up, *proposed* until
Ege signs off; the default is unchanged.
